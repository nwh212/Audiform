Audiform is based on Altered Audio Editor by Steve Phillips and The
GeekCast Radio Network, LLC. That project's name and logo are its own
trademarks and are not used here; this pack is Audiform's own artwork.

icon/
  icon-1024.png   Master app icon. In the repo, run: npx tauri icon brand/icon/icon-1024.png -o src-tauri/icons
  icon.ico        Windows icon with the simplified glyph at 16 and 32 px and the full icon at 48 to 256 px.
                  After running "tauri icon", copy this over src-tauri/icons/icon.ico to keep the sharper small sizes.
  icon.svg        Full icon, vector: a sound wave inside the rings of the 3D Space room.
  icon-small.svg  Simplified glyph for 32 px and below.
  icon-16.png, icon-32.png  Simplified glyph at small sizes.

logo/
  logo-dark.png, logo-light.png  1440 x 520, for the README, the website and the About dialog.
  logo-dark.svg, logo-light.svg  Vector versions. The text needs the Inter and Inter Display fonts
                                 (free, SIL Open Font License) to display correctly.
