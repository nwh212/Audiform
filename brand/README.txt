Audiform is based on Altered Audio Editor by Steve Phillips and The
GeekCast Radio Network, LLC. That project's name and logo are its own
trademarks and are not used here; this pack is Audiform's own artwork.

The mark (a large glass wave arching over a smaller one, in blue, violet,
pink, gold and orange, with leaves) was designed by the Audiform project
lead, Broman (GitHub nwh212).

mark/
  audiform-mark.png  The mark alone, full size, transparent background. The source for everything below.

icon/
  icon-1024.png   Master app icon: the mark centered on a transparent square.
                  In the repo, run: npx tauri icon brand/icon/icon-1024.png -o src-tauri/icons
                  (then delete the android and ios folders it makes).
  icon.ico        Windows icon, 16 to 256 px.
  icon-16.png, icon-32.png  Small sizes.

logo/
  logo-dark.png, logo-light.png  1440 x 520: the mark and the "Audiform" wordmark (Inter Display
                                 SemiBold) with "multitrack audio editor" below. For the README,
                                 the website and the About dialog.
